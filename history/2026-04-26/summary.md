Overnight I ran 50 autoresearch iterations and kept 2 that moved the overall score from 0.4286 to 0.7143. The winning insight was deceptively simple: our shop discovery was only scanning the first page of search results, which meant any shop whose best product ranked outside the top 10 was invisible to us. Iterations 32 and 49 progressively widened that search window from 10 to 30 candidates, and shop score jumped from 0.4 to 1.0 on our sample set as a result. Product and voucher categories still have room to grow — sitting at 0.5 and 0.2 respectively — so those are where I will focus the next run.
